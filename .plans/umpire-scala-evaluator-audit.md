# Audit: the Scala munit tests and the Go tests over the IR

fn-113 R14. Each munit test under `model/umpire/test` and `model/temporal/test` is listed with the claim it makes and one outcome:

- **A**: an existing Go test over the IR asserts the same claim.
- **B**: this audit adds a Go assertion. A provisional B checks a hand-written IR copy of a declaration the gate does not lift yet. Its fixture comes from the IR through `Load`, `Build`, `Check` or `NewRealizer`, never from a typed constructor.
- **C**: the claim no longer applies, and the entry says why.

Tasks 7 and 8 delete tests by the suite and line given here. Every Go test named below is in `tools/umpire/model`. The B tests are in `declarations_pins_test.go`, `nexus_pins_test.go` and `activity_pins_test.go`.

**Count.** The tree has 35 munit tests. The spec says 32 and the task says 33. `Declarations.test.scala` holds 18 tests, not 16: the two monitor-watched Query refusals at lines 367 and 382 open with a multi-line `test(`. The 35 are 18 in `Declarations.test.scala`, 9 in `NexusCallerPins.test.scala`, 6 in `StandaloneActivityPins.test.scala` and 2 in `NexusKernel.test.scala`.

**Outcome totals.** A: 6. B: 20. C: 9. Three of the B outcomes (`NexusCallerPins.test.scala:156`, `:257` and `:300`) are provisional; see the open item at the end.

**Baseline.** `TestMigrationGoldens` in `tools/umpire/model`, reading `testdata/migration/{semantics,declarations,refined-properties,inputs}`, freezes every lifted file's complete tables, Definition IDs, fingerprints, refinement rows, Property rows and Query receipts. It backs every A and B entry about `nexus-caller.json` and `activity.json`. The B tests state the specific numbers and rows the Scala pins named, so a reader sees each claim without decoding a golden.
