## What changed
1. **Claim cap removed.** `umpire_dsl::__lifted` gained:
   - `Evaluates<M>`: judge invariant, postcondition or progress number `index`;
   - `Callbacks<W, M>`;
   - `Interprets::__umpire_callbacks::<W>()`.
   
   `#[machine]` (`lifted.rs`, off wasm32) generates exactly one non-capturing closure per claim, generic over `W`. `Interpreted` implements `Evaluates` and registers those callbacks. The const-generic 64-entry tables and `CLAIM_LIMIT` are gone. A tree whose claim counts differ from the compiled registration is refused ("the tree has N invariants; the compiled Model registers M").
   - Expansion snapshots are byte-identical, because the impl is emitted by `lifted::embed`, which the snapshot harness does not render.
   - Control `fixtures/crowded.rs`: 70 invariants, postconditions and progress claims, generated by `macro_rules`. It interprets and agrees.
2. **Hot path**, after profiling Kettle with `perf` (`perf.data`, `perf2.data`):
   - **Fields.** About 21% of interpreted time went to malloc, free, `drop_glue` and `Vec::extend` for the per-frame field and next-state caches. The frame now reads fields through the generated accessors every time. Definitions are still memoised.
   - **Guards.** Each arm re-evaluated every earlier guard, because the front lowers guards to `!(earlier) && written`. `written_guards` recovers the written guard by structural match and falls back to the effective guard otherwise. This is equivalent: arms are tried in order, so every earlier written guard was already evaluated, without failing, to false.
   - No IR or author-visible change.
   - Remaining profile: `eval` about 12%; `Value` drop and malloc/free about 13%, mostly the progress assumption clone per state (the compiled Model also builds its assumption each time) and locals per arm.
3. **Switch.**
   - `fixture!` `run()`, for both the bound and plain forms, goes through `__checked!`. It autoref-probes the World: an admitted Model renders its checker files from `Interpreted` (with the negative claim adapted), and a refused Model renders from the compiled Model.
   - TLC agreement (`check`), the guest and typed replay stay compiled.
   - The `interpreted()` differential hook and the runner's `interpreted_agrees_with_compiled` stay in the gate.
   - `Bound::run` is unchanged for its other callers.
4. **Docs.** README (fixture hooks paragraph) and a DESIGN "Interpreted by default" paragraph.

## Decisions taken
- The callback table is generated by `#[machine]`, generic over the judging World through `Evaluates`, rather than a `Claim` constructor carrying a context. This leaves `umpire-dsl`'s `Claim` types and the guest untouched.
- Fields are read without a cache. Seq-heavy Models now read a sequence field each time it is used; none of the admitted Models are slower as a result, and the differential still agrees.
- The switch lives in the fixture runner, chosen per concrete World by autoref probe. `Checker::explore<M>` itself cannot pick an implementation by trait without specialization, so `Checker` stays the engine and the runner chooses the Model it explores.
