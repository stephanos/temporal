Design
- runner/campaign_options.go: private campaignOptions (embedded groups, JSON-nested as Target,
  Search, Limits, Observation, Retention) and campaignRun (options + SupervisorCommand,
  CoordinatorCommand, RunnerBuild, Progress, Preparer, Executor, Replayer, guidancePlan,
  resumePreflight, failure capacity). newCampaignRun is the single CampaignSpec conversion;
  campaignOptions.normalized() owns the strategy default (removed from validateConfig); a zero
  shard stays zero (unsharded identity, recorded by omission). normalizeCampaign adds the resume
  defaults (resumeRequestDefaults) for Explore.
- coordinator.go: coordinatorConfig and both conversion functions removed. coordinatorRequest =
  {Options campaignOptions, SupervisorCommand, RunnerBuild}; the child re-normalizes decoded options.
- runLocal, runIsolated, validateConfig and all strategy/plan/retention/evidence helpers take
  campaignRun. Campaign plan (campaignPlanRecord) and resume (resumeConfiguration) records keep
  their schemas; resume, shard and minimization map their records through newCampaignRun.
- Exported CampaignSpec fields unchanged; 4 unexported fields moved to campaignRun. See
  ../go-interface-changes.md (no consumer migration; regrouping wish deferred to R5 owner).
