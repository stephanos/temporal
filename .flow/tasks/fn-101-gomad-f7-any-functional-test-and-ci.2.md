## Done summary
Fixed the shared GC-scan-order channel behind the ./tests divergences and the in-memory filesystem bound behind the versioning failures, with fixtures, and verified on the affected suites, the runtime tier, the core set and the representative set (darwin/arm64). The full ./tests set is no longer run (user decision); its sharding, merge and caches stay as tooling.

The channel: Go 1.27's `acquirep` records the M that took the P in `p.oldm`, a copy of that M's 8-byte `self` weak handle. Which M takes the P after a runner-syscall hand-off is host timing, so the scan of the P greyed a different handle per run; 8 bytes of scan work moved between drain slices, the assist or worker ending a slice allocated a work buffer or not, and every later page of the heap followed. `%p` in the membership resolver logs then differed between a recording and its replay (nexus-otel seed 17, 6 of 8 replays), and the callbacks and chasm signal suites diverged between fresh repetitions. Found with a buffered per-event log (span allocations, greyings with their slot, drain slices, assists) in a scratch toolchain under the scratchpad's `dbg-gomad3` copy: the first differing event was the greying of one of three 8-byte objects from slot 96 of the `p` (behind its malloc header), the M self handles. `gomadGreyRuntimeStructures` now shades every M's `self` handle and every P's `oldm` handle at mark start (tools/gomad3/toolchain/runtime/overlay/src/runtime/gomad.go). Instrumented chasm seed 17 runs: 3 event-log variants in 8 repetitions before, 7 of 8 identical after.

The bound: `TestVersioningFunctionalSuite` never diverged; its WAL-mode SQLite database passes 16 MiB, the in-memory filesystem refused the write with EFBIG, SQLite reported SQLITE_IOERR_WRITE (778) and every later transaction failed (seed 17), while seed 11 ran past the 10-minute wall budget. `MaximumFileBytes` is now 256 MiB and the total 1 GiB (internal/gomadfs/fs.go); `TestFilesystemHoldsFilesLargerThanSixteenMebibytes` writes past both former bounds.
