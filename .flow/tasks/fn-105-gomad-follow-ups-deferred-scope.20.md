## Done summary
D20 investigated on darwin/arm64 (2026-10-01); nothing is fixed and the skip stays. Cause: the test's timing arithmetic, not a server or runtime defect. `TestWorkflowTaskHeartbeatingWithEmptyResult` sends a heartbeat every second against a 5 s `WorkflowTaskHeartbeatTimeout`, and the server rejects only strictly after the deadline (`Now().After(OriginalScheduledTime + timeout)`, `respondworkflowtaskcompleted/api.go:322-324`). Natively the heartbeat due at 5 s is 10.3 to 92.3 ms late in 13 runs and is rejected, so both expected rejections rest on round-trip latency and sleep overshoot; a native run with 995 ms sleeps fails 2 of 3 times. Under the strict tick it arrives at exactly chain + 5.000000000 s and is accepted, the rejection moves one heartbeat later, and the loop ends with one rejection on leaf seeds 1 to 17 and on seeds 11 and 17 in the suite. Under `forward` the test passes on the tick offset (1.4 ms at the smallest).

Proposed correction, owned by the owner of `tests/workflow_task_test.go` and undecided: send the two heartbeats that must be rejected 100 ms after a deadline the test computes and assert every heartbeat's outcome (`fn105-d20-variant-t-test.diff.txt`). In a throwaway copy it passes strict leaf seeds 1 to 64, forward leaf seeds 1 to 16, `qualify-set` on seeds 11 and 17 with the skip lifted under both ticks with exact choice-tape replay, native leaf 8 of 8, and native suite 18 of 18 subtest runs, with the expected history unchanged. It removes the need for latency in the rejections and keeps the existing latency ceiling of about 0.93 s for accepted heartbeats. Alternatives recorded with owners: `clock_tick: forward` for the suite (Gomad qualification configuration; qualified here) and an inclusive server comparison (history service, production review; qualified under strict here).
