## Goal and owner decision

Finish all acceptance left open by the Rust host port on the implementation produced by fn-242 and the complete fn-240 extraction scope. The owner requested cleanup first on2026-10-24: finish the narrow fn-232.30 checkpoint, shrink and port the qualification driver, extract the smaller Rust crates, then execute the remaining qualification in a separate spec. This spec is the sole execution owner of that remaining work. Original fn-232 requirements remain open until actual receipts are reconciled; moving ownership supplies no qualification pass.

The implementation prerequisite is completed fn-242 implementation/cutover plus every approved fn-240 extraction/layout task, not just the first two cuts. fn-245 remains separately ordered work and is not an additional prerequisite here. Focused behavior, conformance, boundary and equivalence checks continue during cleanup. Expensive end-to-end qualification follows cleanup. Within this spec, reach-port task .1 finishes before payload-bound task .2 starts because both can change replay preflight and inspection; .2 re-anchors the extracted paths after that integration. Cell-budget task .3 can proceed independently.

## Preserved contracts

Receive fn-232 tasks .13, .14, .22-.25, .31 and .32, and the actual full-cycle execution formerly owned by fn-242 R6/task .8. Preserve the original requirement IDs in the transfer crosswalk. The producer runs once on one genuinely bound final candidate; independent receivers authenticate its raw outputs. The original145 obligations remain mapped through fn-242's documented deduplication, and every original96 stage retains its declared commands, assertions and bounds. A redundant check may be folded under fn-242's named-failure/deletion evidence; no underlying behavioral or resource obligation disappears.

There is no successful fn-232 closing cycle to use as a green reference. Preserve failed cycles as history. During cleanup, compare the preserved stage plan, independent expectations, real fixtures and mutation outcomes. Final acceptance requires the actual new run. Preserve the independently usable frozen Go baseline, original source/compiler/helper inputs and raw evidence until all pending public-verb and R4 consumers have completed their comparisons.

No synthetic success, identity normalization, old-artifact migration or regeneration of behavioral goldens can fill a missing result. Exception (owner, 2026-10-24): goldens and identities whose bytes change only because of fn-247 (protobuf host-side evidence formats and `flate2` compression) or because CLI message text moved to `clap`/`thiserror` (fn-240) are regenerated once, before the fn-246 candidate is bound, and carry no qualification credit by themselves. New source, crate layouts, helper/model implementations and runtime preparations require genuine new bindings. Expected deliberate application failures stay distinct from capacity, infrastructure and replay failures.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **R1:** A complete crosswalk assigns every unfinished source obligation from fn-232 .13/.14/.22-.25/.31/.32 and fn-242 R6/.8 to an execution task and actual receiving proof. Original owners remain incomplete until their own acceptance is proved. Errors: an omitted obligation, duplicate qualification producer or administrative transfer credited as a pass is a finding.
- **R2:** Every original behavioral reach entry is mapped without gap or partial status, with its original assertions/bounds or a justified Go-implementation-only disposition. The strict reach reader rejects deliberate gap/partial mutations; the final cycle executes every required mapped evidence ID, including feature-gated cases. Errors: omitted callable coverage, unresolved collisions, an unexecuted mapped ID or acceptance based only on Flow status refuses completion.
- **R3:** Every fixed retained-payload caller bound is inventoried and either derived from authenticated owning limits or documented as the actual contract. The same payload has one consistent admission contract across consumers. Focused fixtures accept valid declared bounds and refuse one-byte-over bounds with the existing classification; module/identity/pinned-handle guards remain. Errors: an unexplained literal, inconsistent consumer bounds, unbounded read or relaxed generic descriptor check is a finding.
- **R4:** SQLite and multi-instance cells have retained per-owner native transition-byte/call measurements on the current implementation. Confirm their32MiB budgets with headroom or select a measured cell-local budget under the existing authorized policy, updating producer/reader/docs together. Both standalone success/canary paths pass before the full cycle. Diagnostic headroom runs earn no qualification credit. Preserve generic1MiB refusal regressions, the explicit measured8MiB onebox budget and other workload limits. Errors: unmeasured budget changes, substituted timers/retries or missing owner accounting refuse completion.
- **R5:** Every role, native measurement chain, SQLite oracle, installation, runtime overlay/module, live/inspection/provider input and R4 helper selected for final execution is produced or validly admitted from the actual current source, compiler, context and policies. Preserve original reference provenance separately. Count/export/replay bounds and identities agree. Errors: stale source closure, mislabeled role, forged command journal, mismatched default-build bytes or a source change during the bound run refuses admission.
- **R6:** The supported Rust cycle verb runs the complete96-stage producer and required145-obligation final validation on that candidate, retaining actual commands, process outcomes, raw artifacts and identities. Preserve the3600-second policy and existing timing-only deferral; correctness/resource/replay failures stay binding. At most TWO new full driver dispatches are allowed across candidate changes and process restarts; both remain unused at transfer. Early failure or abort consumes a dispatch; standalone diagnostics do not. Ambiguous spawn state remains charged, missing/mismatched journal bindings cannot reset history, and required45GiB HOST/20GiB overlay plus12GiB inner admission are checked at dispatch. After two unsuccessful full attempts, stop patching stages and launching cycles, reassess the architecture and report a revised approach. Errors: missing/nonpassing/duplicate/synthetic stages, unexecuted obligations, reset attempt accounting or a third unreviewed dispatch refuse completion.
- **R7:** Independent receiving proves32x25 fresh runtime instances,32 forced full replays, all ten mandatory workload cells, the required20 retained observed replays, operator/restart/detached inspection and replay, and the three-node120-second ringpop run plus fresh exact replay. All proof binds to the same cycle and real raw results. Reconcile fn-228/fn-219/fn-227/fn-188/fn-223 transfers and fn-235 R5 without rewriting history. Errors: missing/skipped/failed cells, infrastructure failure relabeled as a canary or mismatched lineage earns no credit.
- **R8:** Independent receiving proves R4 release/reference behavior, runtime-cost-equivalent behavior and both qualified workload comparisons with original choices, WASI/network records and stdout/stderr; all18 public verbs pass their own-lineage comparisons and raw-integrity checks. Verify Go retirement/default-set/import/workspace protection and release exclusions. Append final custody/identity records and an informational `tokei` size record. Errors: disposition paperwork substituted for behavior, premature baseline deletion, unexplained rotations or a changed behavior golden refuses completion.
- **R9:** A final requirement-by-requirement audit authenticates the actual results and reconciles them into the original owners, including fn-185 R4, fn-235 R5 and fn-232 tasks, before closing this spec. Source tasks may close only when their original acceptance is proved; fn-242's implementation handoff remains explicitly distinct from this qualification. Errors: receipt hashes without semantic inspection, claims beyond tested scope or an outstanding transferred obligation prevent closure.

## Boundaries

No new feature development, crate extraction or driver rewrite belongs here; fn-242 and fn-240 own those changes. Residual reach ports, the inherited caller-bound repair and measured cell-budget corrections are implementation work retained from fn-232. The full validation may expose further defects; fix within the two-attempt exit rule, then reassess rather than extending the patch loop. No CI dispatch, push or PR is authorized.
