## Edge Cases & Constraints

- The schedule-to-close budget spans attempts; start-to-close and heartbeat budgets reset on each `Poll`; `Tick` with no live budget is disabled. The retained retry laws hold unchanged in atomic Worlds and without cooperative pending cancellation: retryable failure retries exactly below maximum_attempts and otherwise closes Failed; nonretryable failure closes Failed at every attempt. With cooperative pending cancellation, retryable failure closes Canceled at the current attempt, including at the cap, and starts no retry. This boundary-precedence rule is an independently chosen policy, not an inferred server guarantee.
- With asynchronous cancellation, Cancel in Started records a pending request. Heartbeat reports that request without closing; completion/failure remain permitted races. Explicit current-attempt worker acknowledgement closes as Canceled. Pending retryable failure and selected StartToClose/Heartbeat expiry also close as Canceled at the attempt boundary. Selected ScheduleToClose expiry yields TimedOut. A fresh distinct pending Cancel rejects with FailedPrecondition and preserves all state; this is the chosen invalid-state interpretation, while same-request-ID replay remains unspecified. Terminal closure clears pending state and budgets. Atomic cancellation remains available to the offline compatibility World. Live cancellation mode comes from the explicit protocol binding, never an invented Start option or silent default.
- `Some(Ticks(0))` fails the explicit configuration boundaries and source binding; raw `World::new` remains infallible. A one-tick armed budget expires on its first decrement without underflow. No armed nonterminal zero may deadlock instead of expiring.
- Every Model without facts or optional values keeps byte-identical goldens and expansion snapshots; old unit-handler expansions stay exact.
- R30 budgets hold: inner loop under 10 s and gate under the current soft target warm; the activity's default fixture World is bounded so the native checker finishes well inside them, and the larger Worlds are exercised through TLC under `UMPIRE_BACKENDS=require`.
- No new crates beyond what the workspace already admits; the fn-210 R18 dependency policy applies.
