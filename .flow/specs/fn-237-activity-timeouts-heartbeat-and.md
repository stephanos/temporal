## Edge Cases & Constraints

- The schedule-to-close budget spans attempts; start-to-close and heartbeat budgets reset on each accepted `Poll`; `Tick` with no live budget is disabled. Without cooperative pending cancellation, retryable worker failure retries exactly below maximum_attempts and otherwise closes Failed; nonretryable failure closes Failed at every attempt. With cooperative pending cancellation, worker failure closes Failed at the current attempt regardless of retryability or cap, and starts no retry. Nonpending StartToClose/Heartbeat expiry retries below the cap while the spanning ScheduleToClose budget permits; exhausted attempts close TimedOut. Retry preserves the decremented global budget, increments once, rearms waiting and clears attempt timers. These are the corrections delivered by fn-246.15 and approved for fn-246.13, not new policy choices in this spec.
- With asynchronous cancellation, Cancel in Started records a pending request. Heartbeat reports that request without closing; completion/failure remain permitted races. Explicit current-attempt worker acknowledgement and cooperative Scheduled cancellation close as Canceled. Pending worker failure closes Failed; selected pending StartToClose/Heartbeat expiry closes TimedOut, suppressing retry. Selected ScheduleToStart/ScheduleToClose expiry yields TimedOut. A fresh distinct pending Cancel rejects with FailedPrecondition and preserves all state under the retained invalid-state interpretation; same-request-ID replay remains unspecified. Terminal closure clears pending state and budgets. Atomic cancellation remains available to the offline compatibility World. Live cancellation mode comes from the explicit protocol binding, never an invented Start option or silent default.
- `Some(Ticks(0))` fails the explicit configuration boundaries and source binding; raw `World::new` remains infallible. A one-tick armed budget expires on its first decrement without underflow. No armed nonterminal zero may deadlock instead of expiring.
- Every Model without facts or optional values keeps the same checking results and unit-handler semantics; goldens and expansion snapshots may be re-accepted after review.
- R30 soft targets remain an inner loop under 10 s and a warm gate under 60 s. Retain actual timings and all misses; task .3’s historical model-time gate took 367.163 s. Exercise the selected larger Worlds through TLC under `UMPIRE_BACKENDS=require`; a passing command does not establish a timing target.
