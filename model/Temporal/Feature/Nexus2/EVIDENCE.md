## Trust, warning, and performance boundary

The production and authoring declarations contain no `sorry`, `admit`, custom axiom, or new native
extraction. The transitive `#print axioms` inventory in `AuthoringTests.lean` reports only the
established `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` baseline for trust-bearing results.
`native_decide` appears only in isolated computational test theorems and is not evidence used to
construct a checked declaration. The final task gate log is the warning and lint record.

The fair admission comparison forces the same checker work on each side. One warm Lean 4.33.1 run
measured constructor/frontend Property at 0.011164/0.011509 seconds for 1x and
0.012127/0.012066 seconds for 10x; Behavior at 0.006103/0.006074 and 0.006932/0.006810 seconds;
Query at 0.050297/0.049900 and 0.050475/0.050952 seconds. The repeated fixtures may share work, and
startup dominates these small values, so they are neither a scaling curve nor a responsiveness or
human-usability guarantee. The earlier declaration-elaboration table performs unequal work and is
retained only as syntax-overhead data, not as the admission/default decision.
